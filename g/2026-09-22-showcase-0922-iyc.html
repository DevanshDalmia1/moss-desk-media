How to read the Treasury curve in Moss's Yield curve view (IYC)

Moss's Yield curve view (IYC) shows the US Treasury par curve on an iPhone: 11 tenors from 1M to 30Y with rate, one-day change and the date each figure is as of, plus three computed spreads. Use it to see where on the curve a move sits, then continue to Rates (GGR) and Markets (MKT).

What it answers

the treasury curve, its vintages and spreads

How to open it

Type IYC. The walk in the recording: IYC → GGR → MKT.

In action

Each stop opens from a fresh launch; the data is whatever the capture time showed.

What you see

Step by step

  1. Open Yield curve by its route, IYC. The Tenors tab lists 11 rows, 1M to 30Y, with Rate, Chg 1D and As Of columns, and Derived Spreads (3m10y, 2s10s, 5s30s) below. Read As Of first: at our capture (11:29 AM, 22 Sep) every row read Sep 18.
  2. Open Rates by its route, GGR. A Ticker, Level, Last Chg and 1M Bp table in two sections: Treasury Curve (3-month, 2-, 10- and 30-year, and the 10-year real yield DFII10) and Policy & Money (fed funds, SOFR, IORB, prime and the 30-year mortgage rate), with a notable change highlighted in amber.
  3. Open Markets by its route, MKT. Sections stack Today, Indices and Rates, with FX below the fold; each section header links to its full view.

What it offers

  • 11 Treasury tenors, 1M to 30Y, in one four-column table
  • An As Of date on every row, separate from the updated time under the title
  • Derived Spreads computed for you: 3m10y, 2s10s and 5s30s, each with its one-day change
  • Green for rises and red for falls in the change column
  • Tenors, Curve and Series tabs at the top of the view
  • Share and info buttons at the top right of every view
